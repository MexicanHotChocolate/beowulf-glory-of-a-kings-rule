3D models, sky and sound added in round 7 (all listed in the Works Cited on the site):
* Sword: "Antique Estoc" by James Ray Cock and Ulan Cabanilla, Poly Haven, CC0 (`assets/web/models/sword-estoc.glb`, turned point-down and given smaller textures in Blender).
* Dragon: round 7 used the Stanford Dragon scan (CC BY 4.0). Round 9 replaced it with a dragon built in code (`js/wyrm.js`), so the scan is no longer on the site or in the Works Cited.
* Sky: "Venice Sunset" HDRI, Poly Haven, CC0 (`assets/web/env/venice-sunset-1k.hdr`).
* Alarm: `assets/audio/alarm-danger.mp3`, supplied by our group. Source still to add.
* Font in the club: Unbounded (Google Fonts, SIL Open Font License).

Tool credit (not an image, but worth a line): the depth maps that make the paintings move in layers were made with Depth Anything V2 (Yang et al., 2024), using the small model, which is released under the Apache 2.0 license.
